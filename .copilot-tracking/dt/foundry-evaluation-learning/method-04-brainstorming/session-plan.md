# Method 4 brainstorming session plan

## Status and purpose

The user explicitly approved bounded Method 3 closure and starting Method 4 on 2026-11-05. The normal synthesis gate is only partly met. This session explores alternative learning experiences from the accepted working baseline; it does not approve architecture or implementation.

## Design question

How might we give consultants, delivery engineers, and architects hands-on Foundry tooling experiences that teach evaluation concepts and their use during development and operation?

## Inputs

- [Problem definition](../method-03-synthesis/problem-definition.md): audience, role needs, outcomes, and evidence limits.
- [Design questions](../method-03-synthesis/how-might-we-questions.md): eight prompts for exploration.
- [Draft PRD](../prd.md): four goals and proposed requirements, not a selected teaching approach.

## Constraints

- Use Microsoft Foundry evaluation features hands-on; do not require a third-party evaluation platform.
- Keep core work possible without Python authoring or completing another track. Supplied scripts are allowed.
- Use synthetic flight-disruption requests, fictional policy, and read-only tools. Airline expertise is not required.
- Include development feedback and hands-on production investigation using the lab agent. Keep reusable evaluation assets and handover as professional context, not a separate delivery simulation.
- Follow the existing repository's learning-lab purpose. Do not require real customer delivery, customer role-play, or agreement meetings.
- Keep CI/CD execution optional. Optimizer and controlled red teaming remain secondary.
- Preserve individual and facilitated use. The requester confirmed a lab target below 120 minutes after infrastructure provisioning pre-work and aims for approximately 80% feature coverage; exact coverage scope and spend budget remain open.
- Do not turn unverified product support into a promised exercise. No cloud runs or implementation occur during brainstorming.

## Session structure

1. Generate rough one-line ideas from different stakeholder perspectives and constraints.
1. Use AI as Prep + Synthesis: begin with a few labelled coach seeds, invite user ideas, and use AI to help find patterns only after breadth is sufficient.
1. Generate at least 15 ideas before evaluating any. Aim for 4-6 distinct learning approaches; these are not predetermined module categories.
1. Rotate perspectives across consultant, delivery engineer, architect, operator/successor, and independent learner/facilitator.
1. When the target is met and the user is ready, group ideas into 3-5 themes by learning approach, with rationale.
1. Carry several themes into Method 5 rather than select a module sequence here.
